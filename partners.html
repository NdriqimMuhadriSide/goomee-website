Partner with Goomee

For restaurants, venues, event organisers, places to stay and booking platforms.

Goomee suggests places to people at the moment they are deciding where to go: a table for tonight, a museum for a rainy afternoon, a concert nearby, somewhere to stay. We launch first in Belgium, with Brussels at the start.

How partnering works

  • Booking links. When Goomee recommends you, travellers can book through your own site or your booking partner. If you run an affiliate programme, we link through it and earn a commission only on bookings that are actually made.
  • An accurate listing. Tell us your opening hours, prices and what makes you worth the visit, and we keep your details right.
  • Events and experiences. Share what is coming up, so we can suggest it to people nearby at the right moment.

What we will never sell

Recommendations are ranked for the traveller. A commission never decides what we recommend, and it never changes the price anyone pays. If we ever show a paid placement, it is clearly labelled Sponsored.
