3. Location

Goomee asks for location only while the app is open. There is no background location tracking.

Your precise coordinates are used to answer the question you are asking — what is good near you, right now — and are then discarded. For caching we round your position to a coarse grid cell rather than storing your exact position against your account, so the record of where you were is approximate by design.

Weather information is requested using that same coarsened cell, not your precise position, and carries no account information.

If you turn on recommendation notifications, we keep the area you last used the app in, rounded to about 1 km, so that an evening suggestion is for somewhere near you. It is replaced each time you use the app, and removed with the device when you turn notifications off or delete your account.

4. Personalisation and the AI planner

If you enable personalisation, we build a taste profile from what you save, open and skip, and use it to rank what you are shown. This is profiling in the sense of the GDPR. It affects the order of recommendations. It produces no legal or similarly significant effects, and there is no automated decision-making of the kind described in Article 22.

You can reset your taste profile at any time from the app, which erases the signals behind it, or switch personalisation off entirely and keep using Goomee.

My Goomee Planner generates itineraries using Google’s Gemini models on Google Cloud. The trip details you provide are sent to that service to produce the itinerary. We do not send your email address, name or account identifier.

6. Transfers outside the EEA

Our database and API are hosted in the EU. Some providers, in particular Google and Stripe, may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens it is covered by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and the provider’s own transfer safeguards. You can ask us for details of the safeguards in place.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account data — until you delete your account.
  • Saved places, history and trips — until you delete them, or your account.
  • Product analytics — 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Recommendation records — 30 days.
  • Contact messages — as long as needed to handle your question, then deleted.
  • Technical caches — expire automatically; place data is refreshed at least every 30 days.
  • Consent records — kept for as long as we need to demonstrate that consent was given, as the GDPR requires.

Deleting your account removes your profile, your saved places, your history, your trips, your profile picture and the signals behind your taste profile.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a portable copy, object to processing based on legitimate interests, ask us to restrict processing, and withdraw consent.

Two of these are built into the app and need no request:

  • Download everything we hold about you — a single JSON file, including a link to your profile picture.
  • Delete your account — immediate and permanent.

For anything else, email goomee.support@gmail.com. We respond within one month, as the GDPR requires.

9. Security

Your data is isolated at the database level: every row carrying personal data is protected by a policy that allows access only to the account that owns it, enforced by the database rather than by application code remembering to check. Traffic is encrypted in transit. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach us.

10. Cookies and statistics on this website

This website sets no cookies. To see how many people visit and which pages they read, we use Vercel Web Analytics, which works without cookies and does not identify you: visits are only counted in aggregate, no profile is built of you, and nothing is shared with advertisers. That is why you were not asked to accept anything.

The site also remembers the language and the light or dark theme you choose, in your browser’s own storage. That stays on your device and is never sent to us.

11. Children

Goomee is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for anything significant we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
